### **Typography: Modern + Legible**

**Heading Font:**
- **Inter** eller **Poppins** (geometric sans-serif)
- Bold weights (600-700) för impact
- Large sizes (48-72px för hero H1)
- Spacing: Generous line-height (1.2-1.4)

**Body Font:**
- **Inter** eller **Open Sans** (clean, readable)
- Regular weight (400) för body, Medium (500) för emphasis
- Size: 16-18px (läsbar på alla enheter)
- Spacing: 1.6-1.8 line-height för komfort

**Accent Font (optional):**
- **Space Grotesk** eller **Archivo** för "Beta Program" badges
- Uppercase + letterspacing för premium feel

## MOBILE-FIRST CONSIDERATIONS

**Critical:** 70% of traffic will be mobile.

**Mobile Optimizations:**
- **Hero headline:** Shorter (2 lines max on mobile)
- **Font sizes:** 36px for H1 on mobile (48-72px on desktop)
- **CTAs:** Thumb-friendly (minimum 44px height)
- **Images:** Load fast (optimized WebP, lazy loading)
- **Sections:** Stacked vertically, no side-by-side columns on small screens
- **Form:** Single column, large inputs
- **Sticky CTA:** Consider sticky "Säkra Din Plats" button at bottom on scroll

## INTERACTIONS & MICRO-ANIMATIONS

**Subtle, not distracting:**

1. **Scroll-triggered animations:**
   - Fade in sections as user scrolls (Intersection Observer API)
   - Counters animate up ("37 platser kvar" counts from 81 to 37)

2. **Hover states:**
   - Buttons: Subtle lift (transform: translateY(-2px)) + darker shade
   - Cards: Subtle shadow increase on hover
   - Links: Underline slide-in effect

3. **Loading states:**
   - Form submission: Button changes to "Registrerar..." with spinner
   - Success: Green checkmark animation + "Du är registrerad! Check din email."

4. **DNA helix animation (hero):**
   - Slow rotation (10-15 seconds per rotation)
   - Subtle glow effect (pulsing)
   - NOT distracting, just alive

**No:**
- ❌ Parallax scrolling (can feel gimmicky)
- ❌ Auto-playing videos (annoying)
- ❌ Pop-ups on entry (kills trust)
- ❌ Countdown timers (feels scammy)

---

## COPYWRITING TONE

**Voice:** Smart friend who gets it, not salesperson

**Do:**
- ✅ Use "Du" (casual Swedish, not "Ni")
- ✅ Short sentences. Punchy.
- ✅ Speak benefits, not features ("Inga fler slösade pengar" not "DNA-analys av 70 gener")
- ✅ Honesty ("Vi är en startup" not pretend we're huge)
- ✅ Scientific but accessible ("VDR Fok1-genen" with simple explanation)

**Don't:**
- ❌ Overpromise ("Cure your fatigue!" - nej)
- ❌ Fear-mongering ("You're poisoning yourself!" - nej)
- ❌ Fake urgency ("Only 3 hours left!" when not true)
- ❌ Corporate jargon ("Leverage synergistic solutions" - nej)

**Examples:**

**Good:**
"Din DNA visar om du kan ta upp D-vitamin. Sluta gissa."

**Bad:**
"Our proprietary nutrigenomic analysis leverages cutting-edge biotechnology
to optimize your micronutrient absorption efficacy."

## WHAT TO AVOID (Anti-Patterns)

**Don't look like:**
- ❌ Cheesy supplement MLM sites (bright colors, stock photos of pills)
- ❌ "Get rich quick" landing pages (fake testimonials, countdown timers)
- ❌ 23andMe (too clinical, cold, not wellness-focused)

**Avoid:**
- ❌ Too much text (people skim, not read)
- ❌ Too many CTAs (decision paralysis)
- ❌ Stock photos of doctors in white coats (cliché)
- ❌ Tiny unreadable text for legal disclaimers
