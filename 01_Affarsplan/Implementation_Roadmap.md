# RISK MITIGATION PLAN

## Risk 1: ISO 27001 tar längre tid (>6 månader)
**Mitigation:**
- Start parallel med MVP (månad 1)
- Extern konsult (experienced)
- Buffer 2 månader (launch månad 8 worst case)
- **Impact:** Delay launch, men compliance fortfarande klar before scale

## Risk 2: DNA-pris 5 995 SEK för högt (low conversion)
**Mitigation:**
- Beta-test @ 4 995 SEK (månad 1-6)
- A/B test landing page
- Klarna betalplan (1 495 down + 12×375)
- **Pivot:** Om <50% av beta sell-through → sänk till 4 495 SEK, kompensera med högre volume

## Risk 3: Serie Seed Extension fails (månad 15)
**Mitigation:**
- Start fundraise månad 13 (2 månader lead time)
- Parallel bridge financing option
- Cost cuts: Reduce marketing 50K → 30K, extend runway
- **Impact:** Slower growth År 2, break-even År 3 delayed to Q2

## Risk 4: Lab quality issues (DNA reports inaccurate)
**Mitigation:**
- Dual supplier (Eurofins 70% + Nordic Labs 30%)
- QA spot checks (10% of reports)
- Dietist review ALWAYS (catches errors)
- **Escalation:** Switch to 50/50 if one lab problematic

## Risk 5: AI-dietist quality concerns (customers unhappy)
**Mitigation:**
- Dietist review ALWAYS (not optional)
- Feedback loop: Customer satisfaction survey
- Continuous training: Update RAG database quarterly
- **Pivot:** If satisfaction <80% → increase dietist review time (30 min vs 15 min), sacrifice scale for quality

## Risk 6: GDPR breach / security incident
**Mitigation:**
- ISO 27001 compliance
- Incident response retainer (330K/år)
- Pen-testing 2×/år
- Cyber insurance (60K/år)
- **Response:** <72h notification to IMY, forensics, remediation, customer notification

## Risk 7: Regulatory change (IVDR enforcement)
**Mitigation:**
- Legal retainer (120K/år) monitors changes
- Wellness positioning documented (månad 1)
- Advisory board with regulatory expert
- **Pivot:** If IVDR enforced → seek CE marking (12-18 månader, 500K-1M cost) OR pivot to blood-based biomarkers (not genetic)
