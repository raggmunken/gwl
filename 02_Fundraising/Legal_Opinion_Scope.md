## KEY LEGAL QUESTION

### Primary Question:

**"Är Genetic Wellness Labs' tjänst en medicinteknisk produkt enligt IVDR (EU 2017/746)?"**

**If YES:**
- We need CE-märkning (costly, 12+ months)
- Clinical evidence requirements
- Notified body approval
- Cost: +2M SEK

**If NO:**
- We can launch as wellness service (Month 6)
- Self-regulated (Livsmedelsverket for supplements, GDPR for data)
- Cost: Current budget (already allocated)

### Secondary Questions:

**2. "Klassificeras DNA-test som in vitro diagnostic (IVD) under IVDR?"**

**Relevant IVDR Definition:**
> "IVD medical device" = device intended to provide information for diagnostic purposes

**Our Argument:**
- We provide information for **wellness optimization** (not diagnosis)
- Analogt med: 23andMe (ancestry + health insights, not diagnosis)

**But:**
- If we say "you have MTHFR mutation = risk för hjärtsjukdom" → diagnostic claim
- If we say "MTHFR variant = consider methylfolate" → wellness optimization

**3. "Kan Läkemedelsverket tvinga oss att klassificera som medicinteknisk?"**

**Relevant Swedish Law:**
- Läkemedelsverket enforces IVDR in Sweden
- They have wide discretion
- If they say "this is medical device" → we must comply OR stop

**Our Risk:**
- If Läkemedelsverket investigates (complaint, random audit)
- If they disagree with wellness classification
- If they issue "försäljningsförbud" (sales ban)

**4. "Hur strukturerar vi tjänsten för att minimera risk?"**

**Examples:**
- Language: "Optimize" vs "Diagnose"
- Dietist as gatekeeper: Dietist has medical responsibility, not us
- Disclaimers: "Not intended for diagnosis, treatment, or cure"
- No disease claims: Focus on wellness, not disease prevention
