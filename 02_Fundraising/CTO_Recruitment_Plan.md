## WHY WE NEED A CTO (NOT JUST A DEVELOPER)

### Technical Challenges We Face:

1. **Security-First Architecture**
   - ISO 27001 compliance (genetic data = Article 9 GDPR)
   - Encryption at rest & in transit
   - Access controls, audit logs, penetration testing

2. **Healthcare-Specific Requirements**
   - GDPR Article 9 (genetic data)
   - Integration with lab systems (ISO 17025)
   - Dietist workflow (legitimerad dietist must approve all plans)

3. **Scalability**
   - Year 1: 500 customers
   - Year 3: 10,000 customers
   - Must handle genetic data processing at scale

4. **AI/ML Integration**
   - AI-dietist recommendation engine
   - Gene → supplement matching algorithm
   - Continuous learning from customer outcomes

### Why Developer Isn't Enough:

| Aspect | Senior Developer | CTO |
|--------|------------------|-----|
| **Code** | ✅ Writes excellent code | ✅ Writes excellent code |
| **Architecture** | ⚠️ Follows existing patterns | ✅ Designs scalable systems |
| **Security** | ⚠️ Basic security awareness | ✅ ISO 27001 compliance expertise |
| **Team Building** | ❌ Individual contributor | ✅ Can hire and lead tech team |
| **Strategic Thinking** | ❌ Executes on tasks | ✅ Tech roadmap aligned with business |

**Verdict:** We need CTO-level thinking from Month 6-9, not just coding.

## CTO PROFILE: WHO WE'RE LOOKING FOR

### Must-Have Requirements:

#### 1. Technical Skills
- **Backend:** Node.js OR Python (prefer both)
- **Databases:** PostgreSQL, Redis (for caching)
- **Cloud:** AWS (specifically AWS Stockholm for GDPR)
- **Security:** ISO 27001 experience (or equivalent)
- **API Design:** RESTful APIs, authentication, rate limiting

#### 2. Healthcare-Tech Experience
- **MUST have:** Previous role in healthcare SaaS or healthtech startup
- **Ideal:** Worked with sensitive data (PHI, genetic data, financial data)
- **Examples:** Kry, Min Doktor, Doktor.se, Werlabs, Lifesum alumni

#### 3. Compliance & Security
- **Understanding of:** GDPR Article 9 (special category data)
- **Experience with:** ISO 27001, SOC 2, or equivalent security frameworks
- **Bonus:** CISM, CISSP, or security certifications

#### 4. Startup Experience
- **MUST have:** Been at early-stage startup (seed to Series A)
- **Ideal:** Built products from 0 to 1 (not just scaled existing)
- **Comfort with:** Ambiguity, wearing multiple hats, fast iteration

#### 5. Leadership
- **Must be able to:** Hire and manage tech team (we'll need 2-3 developers by Year 2)
- **Communication:** Can explain technical concepts to non-technical stakeholders
- **Ownership:** Takes responsibility, doesn't just execute

### Nice-to-Have (But Not Required):
- AI/ML experience (we'll use managed services initially)
- Frontend skills (we can hire frontend separately)
- Swedish language (English OK, but Swedish helps with dietist integration)

## COMPENSATION PACKAGE

### Equity: 1-2% with 4-Year Vesting

**Structure:**
- **Total equity:** 1-2% (negotiable based on seniority)
- **Vesting:** 4 years with 1-year cliff
  - 25% vests after Year 1
  - 6.25% vests each quarter Year 2-4
- **Acceleration:** None (standard terms)

**Value at Seed Round (Post-Money 27.8M SEK):**
- 1% = 278K SEK
- 2% = 556K SEK

**Value at Exit (150M SEK):**
- 1% = 1.5M SEK
- 2% = 3M SEK

### Cash Salary: 0 SEK Year 1, Market Rate Year 2+

**Year 1 (Month 6-18):**
- **Salary:** 0 SEK (equity-only)
- **Rationale:** Bootstrapping, conserve cash runway
- **For CTO:** "Sweat equity" phase, bet on upside

**Year 2+ (Post-Series A):**
- **Salary:** 600-800K SEK/year (market rate for startup CTO in Stockholm)
- **Rationale:** Once we have Series A capital (Month 18), pay market salary

### Alternative: Lower Equity + Salary (If Needed)

**Option B (If CTO needs cash):**
- **Equity:** 0.5-1% (lower)
- **Salary Year 1:** 300K SEK (half-time equivalent)
- **Salary Year 2+:** 600-800K SEK

**Trade-off:** Less risk for CTO, less upside, but more cash stability.

### Phase 2: Outreach & Interviews (Month 3-6)

**Outreach Template (LinkedIn/Email):**

```
Subject: CTO for Healthtech Startup (DNA + Preventive Health)

Hi [Name],

I'm Rasmus, founder of Genetic Wellness Labs. We're building a preventive health platform that combines DNA-testing, legitimerad dietist, and personalized supplements to help people reach their health goals.

We're pre-seed (securing 5M SEK seed round) and looking for a CTO to join as co-founder-level hire. The role:

I saw your experience at [Company] and [specific project] caught my eye. Would love to chat if this sounds interesting.

15-min intro call?

Best,
Rasmus
```

**Interview Process:**

**Round 1: 30-min Intro Call**
- Culture fit: Why healthtech? Why startup?
- Experience: Walk through past projects
- Availability: When can you start?

**Round 2: 60-min Technical Deep Dive**
- Architecture: How would you design a GDPR-compliant genetic data platform?
- Security: ISO 27001 experience, threat modeling
- Scalability: How to go from 500 to 10K customers?

**Round 3: 30-min Founder Fit**
- Vision alignment: Do we see the future the same way?
- Working style: How do we collaborate?
- Equity discussion: Is 1-2% + 0 SEK Year 1 acceptable?

**Round 4: Reference Checks**
- Talk to 2-3 previous colleagues
- Ask: "Would you work with [Name] again?"

**Timeline:** 4-6 weeks from first contact to offer

## INTERVIEW QUESTIONS (FOR CTO CANDIDATES)

### Technical Questions:

1. **Architecture:**
   - "How would you design a system to store genetic data compliant with GDPR Article 9?"
   - "What's your approach to encryption at rest vs in transit?"

2. **Security:**
   - "Walk me through how you'd implement ISO 27001 for a healthtech startup."
   - "How do you balance security and speed in a startup environment?"

3. **Scalability:**
   - "We need to go from 500 to 10K customers. What breaks first and how do you prevent it?"
   - "When do you start thinking about microservices vs monolith?"

4. **Healthcare-Specific:**
   - "Have you worked with PHI, genetic data, or similarly sensitive data before?"
   - "How do you handle integrations with lab systems (e.g., ISO 17025 certified labs)?"

### Culture Fit Questions:

1. **Startup Experience:**
   - "Tell me about a time you had to ship a product with 20% of the features you wanted. How did you prioritize?"
   - "What's the hardest technical decision you made with incomplete information?"

2. **Leadership:**
   - "When will we need to hire our first developer, and what profile should they have?"
   - "How do you communicate technical trade-offs to non-technical stakeholders?"

3. **Equity & Compensation:**
   - "Are you OK with 0 SEK salary Year 1, equity-only? Why or why not?"
   - "What's your expected equity range for this stage company?"

### Red Flags to Watch For:

- ❌ "I need market salary from Day 1" (not startup-ready)
- ❌ "ISO 27001 is overkill for a startup" (doesn't understand healthcare compliance)
- ❌ "I don't have healthcare experience, but I can learn" (risk too high)
- ❌ "I prefer to work alone" (can't build a team)
