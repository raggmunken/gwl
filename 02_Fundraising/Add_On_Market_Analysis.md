**Strategic Implication:** Wearables = gateway drug to add-on ecosystem
- Wearables integration should be TOP priority (Month 7-9 launch)

## Risk Factors & Mitigation

### Risk 1: Adoption Slower Than Projected

**Mitigation:**
- Conservative financial planning (use 30% of market potential)
- Freemium model (low friction, high awareness)
- Onboarding campaigns (email, in-app nudges)

### Risk 2: High Churn for Digital-Only Add-Ons

**Mitigation:**
- Add human touch elements (quarterly dietist reviews add-on mentions)
- Gamification (streaks, badges for consistent use)
- Results tracking (before/after comparisons)

### Risk 3: Wearables Integration Complexity

**Mitigation:**
- Start with Apple Health + Google Fit (broadest reach)
- Add Oura/Whoop/Garmin Month 9-12 (prioritize by customer requests)
- Partner with wearables companies (co-marketing opportunities)

### Risk 4: PT Partnership Quality Control

**Mitigation:**
- Strict partner selection criteria (>4.5/5 stars, DNA integration training)
- Customer feedback loops (NPS after each session)
- Option to switch PTs (no penalty)
- Monthly PT performance reviews (terminate low performers)
