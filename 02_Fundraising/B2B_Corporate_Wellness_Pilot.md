### Company Size

**Sweet Spot: 50-100 Employees**
- **Why Lower Bound (50):** Too small = HR lacks budget/bandwidth for wellness programs
- **Why Upper Bound (100):** Too large = enterprise sales cycle (6-12 months), need procurement/legal
- **Pilot Focus:** 50-100 (can handle complexity, but decision-maker accessible)

**Future Expansion (Year 3):**
- 100-500 employees (mid-market)
- 500+ (enterprise, requires dedicated sales team)

---

### Industry Verticals

**Tier 1 (Best Fit):**
- **Tech/SaaS:** High salaries, value employee benefits, data-driven culture
  - Examples: Spotify, Klarna, iZettle, Northvolt, Tink
- **Consulting:** Long hours, stress → wellness programs reduce burnout
  - Examples: Boston Consulting Group, McKinsey (Sweden offices)
- **Finance:** High-performance culture, already invest in wellness (gym memberships)
  - Examples: Swedbank, Nordea, SEB

**Tier 2 (Good Fit):**
- **E-commerce:** Fast-growing, young workforce, competitive for talent
  - Examples: Mathem, Oda, Budbee
- **Gaming:** Tech-savvy, performance-focused (sleep, stress, cognitive add-ons appeal)
  - Examples: King, Paradox Interactive, Mojang

**Tier 3 (Challenging):**
- **Manufacturing:** Older workforce, less interest in DNA testing
- **Retail:** Lower margins, hard to justify 8K/employee
- **Healthcare:** Ironic, but skeptical of "wellness" (prefer evidence-based only)

**Pilot Target:** Tech/SaaS (Tier 1)

### Decision Maker

**Primary:** HR Manager or Chief People Officer
- **Title:** "Head of People", "HR Director", "People & Culture Lead"
- **Pain Points:**
  - Employee burnout (sick leave costs 5,000-10,000 SEK/day)
  - Talent retention (replacing employee costs 6-9 months salary)
  - Generic wellness programs (gym memberships, 30% usage)
- **KPIs They Care About:**
  - Sick leave reduction (target: -10-20%)
  - Employee engagement scores (target: >70%)
  - Retention rate (target: >90% annual)

**Secondary:** CFO or CEO (for budget approval)
- **Pain Points:**
  - Wellness ROI unclear ("We spend 500K on gym, does it work?")
  - Want data/metrics (not feel-good fluff)
- **KPIs They Care About:**
  - Cost per employee (<10K/year benchmark for wellness)
  - Productivity impact (hard to measure, but testimonials help)

## 📦 B2B PACKAGE STRUCTURE

### Tier 1: BASIC (8,000 SEK/employee/year)

**Equivalent to:** B2C Essential tier + lite add-ons

**Included:**
- **DNA Test:** 50 SNPs (nutrition-focused)
- **Personalized Supplements:** Basic formula (695 kr/mån value)
- **Nutrition Plan:** Dietist-created baseline
- **2× Group Workshops per Year:**
  - Workshop 1 (Q1): "Nutrition 101 - Eat for Energy"
  - Workshop 2 (Q3): "Sleep & Stress Management"
- **Employee Dashboard:** Track personal progress (DNA results, supplement adherence)

**Not Included:**
- No add-ons (Sleep, Stress, Workout Premium)
- No 1-on-1 dietist calls (group only)
- No blood tests

**Cost Breakdown (per employee):**
- DNA test (one-time): 1,400 SEK (amortized: 117 kr/mån)
- Supplements: 695 kr/mån × 12 = 8,340 SEK
- Workshops: 20K per workshop (for 50 employees) = 400 SEK/employee
- Platform cost: 20 kr/mån × 12 = 240 SEK
- **Total COGS:** 9,097 SEK
- **Margin:** 8,000 - 9,097 = **-1,097 SEK** ❌ **LOSS!**

**Verdict:** Basic tier is **not profitable** at 8K price point.

**Options:**
1. Increase price to 9,500 SEK (margin: 403 SEK/employee)
2. Remove supplements from Basic (DNA + workshops only, price 5,000 SEK)
3. **Recommended:** Eliminate Basic tier, start at Premium (10K)

### Tier 2: PREMIUM (10,000 SEK/employee/year) ✅ RECOMMENDED

**Equivalent to:** B2C Premium tier + 2 add-ons included

**Included:**
- **DNA Test:** 100 SNPs (nutrition + wellness SNPs)
- **Personalized Supplements:** Premium formula (865 kr/mån value)
- **Nutrition Plan:** Dietist-created + 1× quarterly group check-in call
- **2× Add-Ons (Company Chooses):**

  - Q1: "Nutrition for Performance"
  - Q2: "Sleep Optimization for Productivity"
  - Q3: "Stress Resilience at Work"
  - Q4: "Fitness & Movement Basics"
- **HR Dashboard:** Aggregated (anonymized) health metrics
  - Average sleep hours
  - % of employees with vitamin deficiencies
  - Engagement rate (% logging into app monthly)

**Not Included:**
- No 1-on-1 dietist calls (group only)
- No blood tests
- No PT coaching (Workout Premium is digital only)

**Cost Breakdown (per employee):**
- DNA test: 1,400 SEK (one-time, amortized 117 kr/mån)
- Supplements: 865 kr/mån × 12 = 10,380 SEK
- Add-ons (2×): 195 × 2 × 12 = 4,680 SEK (but COGS only 20% = 936 SEK)
- Workshops: 20K × 4 workshops / 50 employees = 1,600 SEK
- Platform: 240 SEK
- **Total COGS:** 14,556 SEK
- **Margin:** 10,000 - 14,556 = **-4,556 SEK** ❌ **STILL LOSS!**

**Problem:** B2B pricing too low vs B2C equivalent (B2C Premium = 14,375 SEK/year).

**Fix:** Increase price to **12,000 SEK/employee/year**
- **Margin:** 12,000 - 14,556 = -2,556 SEK (still loss, but need volume discount on supplements)

**With Bulk Discount (20% COGS reduction for 100+ employees):**
- Supplements COGS: 10,380 × 0.8 = 8,304 SEK
- Total COGS: 1,400 + 8,304 + 936 + 1,600 + 240 = **12,480 SEK**
- **Margin at 12K price:** 12,000 - 12,480 = **-480 SEK** (breakeven!)

**Revised Price:** **12,000 SEK/employee/year** with 20% bulk discount on supplements.

### Pilot Success Metrics

**Metric 1: Employee Enrollment Rate**
- **Target:** ≥80% of employees sign up
- **Why Important:** Low enrollment = program perceived as not valuable
- **How to Measure:** Signups / Total employees invited

**Metric 2: Employee Engagement Rate**
- **Target:** ≥60% log into app ≥1× per month
- **Why Important:** High engagement = using the product (not just signed up)
- **How to Measure:** MAU (Monthly Active Users) / Total enrolled

**Metric 3: Employee Satisfaction (NPS)**
- **Target:** NPS ≥40 (Net Promoter Score)
- **Why Important:** NPS >40 = employees would recommend to others
- **How to Measure:** Survey after 3 months: "How likely are you to recommend GWL to a friend? (0-10)"
  - Promoters (9-10): 60%
  - Passives (7-8): 20%
  - Detractors (0-6): 20%
  - NPS = 60% - 20% = **40**

**Metric 4: HR Satisfaction**
- **Target:** HR rates program ≥4/5 stars
- **Why Important:** HR is renewal decision-maker
- **How to Measure:** End-of-pilot survey (1-5 scale)
  - "How satisfied are you with the pilot?" (5 = very satisfied)
  - "Would you renew for another year?" (Yes/Maybe/No)

**Metric 5: Health Outcomes (Soft Metrics)**
- **Target:** ≥30% of employees report improvement
- **How to Measure:** Survey: "Since starting GWL, have you noticed improvements in: Sleep, Energy, Stress, Nutrition?" (Yes/No for each)
- **Why Important:** Anecdotal, but powerful for testimonials
