## 🎯 EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**Decision:** Partner med befintliga PT-företag istället för att bygga in-house PT team.

**Varför Partnership:**
- ✅ **Snabbare lansering:** 3 månader (vs 9-12 månader in-house)
- ✅ **Lägre risk:** Variabel kostnad (betalar bara för aktiva kunder)
- ✅ **Test market:** Validera efterfrågan innan stora investeringar
- ✅ **Flexibilitet:** Kan skala upp/ner utan att hantera anställda

**Revenue Model:**
- Customer pays: 495 kr/mån (Level 2 Workout Coaching)
- Partner PT gets: 70% (347 kr/mån)
- **We get: 30% (148 kr/mån)** - net margin 26% efter platform cost
- Our cost: 20 kr/mån (platform, integration, support)
- **Net profit: 128 kr/mån per PT customer**

**Year 1 Target:**
- 65 customers (10% av 650 totalt)
- Revenue (our cut): 148 × 65 × 2 månader = **19,240 SEK**
- Year 2 target: 240 customers (20% av 1,200) = **427K årligen**

**Option:** Bring in-house Year 3 om volume >200 customers (economics förbättras).

## 🤔 WHY PARTNERSHIP VS IN-HOUSE?

### In-House Model (EJ VALD)

**Advantages:**
- Higher margin (80-90% vs 30%)
- Full control över quality
- Direct customer relationship
- Data ownership (workout feedback)

**Disadvantages:**
- ❌ **Slow launch:** Recruit → Train → Launch = 6-9 månader
- ❌ **Fixed cost:** 40K/mån per full-time PT (even if 0 customers)
- ❌ **Hiring risk:** Svårt att hitta PT med DNA-knowledge
- ❌ **Scaling challenge:** Måste anställa fler när demand växer
- ❌ **Regulatory:** Arbetsmiljöverket, insurance, HR overhead

**Cost Example (Year 1):**
- 1× PT (full-time): 40K/mån × 12 = 480K
- Benefits + insurance: 20% = 96K
- Training (DNA education): 50K
- **Total: 626K** for capacity to handle 100 customers
- Break-even: 626K ÷ (495 - 50 COGS) ÷ 12 = **117 customers needed**

**Year 1 Reality:** Only 65 customers → **LOSS of 300K**

---

### Partnership Model (✅ VALD)

**Advantages:**
- ✅ **Fast launch:** Partner already has PT pool → 3 månaders integration
- ✅ **Variable cost:** Pay per customer (70% revenue share)
- ✅ **Market validation:** Test demand without big upfront investment
- ✅ **No HR overhead:** Partner handles recruitment, training, payroll
- ✅ **Scalability:** Partner can handle 10 or 1,000 customers
- ✅ **Flexibility:** Can switch partners or bring in-house later

**Disadvantages:**
- Lower margin (30% vs 80%)
- Less control över PT quality (mitigated by contract terms)
- Shared customer relationship (white-label helps)
- Data sharing (DNA data must be API-accessible)

**Cost Example (Year 1):**
- 65 customers × 495 kr/mån × 2 månader = 64,350 SEK revenue
- Partner gets: 70% = 45,045 SEK
- **We get: 30% = 19,305 SEK** ✅
- Our cost: 20 kr/mån × 65 × 2 = 2,600 SEK (platform)
- **Net profit: 16,705 SEK** (vs -300K in-house)

**Break-Even:** Immediate (variable cost structure)

## 🔍 PARTNER SELECTION CRITERIA

### Must-Have Requirements

**1. Digital Coaching Capability:**
- Video call infrastructure (Zoom, Teams, custom platform)
- Experience med remote coaching (not just in-person gym)
- Scheduling system (booking, cancellations, rescheduling)

**2. PT Network Size:**
- Minimum 10 PTs available (to handle 100+ customers)
- PTs located across Sweden (Stockholm, Göteborg, Malmö)
- Mix of specializations (strength, endurance, rehabilitation)

**3. Quality Standards:**
- PT certifications (SATS Academy, IKSU, AFPT, or equivalent)
- Customer satisfaction >4.5/5 stars
- Evidence-based training (not fad diets/workouts)

**4. Technical Integration:**
- Willingness to integrate with our DNA platform (API)
- Accept DNA-based recommendations (some PTs skeptical of genetics)
- White-label option (coaching appears as "Genetic Wellness Labs PT")

**5. Business Terms:**
- Revenue share model (we propose 70/30, negotiate if needed)
- Exclusivity in DNA-wellness market (non-compete 3 years)
- Minimum contract: 12 months (both sides can exit after)

---

- PTs already educated about ACTN3, ACE genes
- Experience with personalized training (not cookie-cutter programs)

**2. Wearables Integration:**
- Already integrate with Garmin, Apple Watch, Whoop

**3. Swedish Market Focus:**
- Not international (want local presence for potential in-person sessions later)
- Understand Swedish wellness culture (lagom, not extreme)

**4. Nutrition Collaboration:**
- Willing to coordinate with our dietist

## 📋 PARTNERSHIP CONTRACT (KEY TERMS)

### Revenue Share

**Standard Model:**
- Customer pays: 495 kr/mån
- Partner PT receives: **70%** (347 kr/mån)
- Genetic Wellness Labs receives: **30%** (148 kr/mån)

**Justification (70/30):**
- PT does the work (coaching, programming, video calls)
- We provide: Customer acquisition, DNA data, platform, payment processing
- Industry standard: 60-80% goes to service provider (coach, therapist)

**Negotiation Flexibility:**
- If partner pushes back, can go 65/35 (we get 173 kr/mån)
- If partner is premium (Pactster), may need 75/25 (we get 124 kr/mån)
- **Never go below 25%** (our CAC is ~1,300 kr, need margin to cover)

---

### Payment Terms

**Billing:**
- Customer pays us: 495 kr/mån (via Klarna, recurring)
- We pay partner: 70% monthly (within 15 days of month-end)
- Partner invoices us: Monthly (no session-by-session)

**Cancellation:**
- Customer cancels → we notify partner within 24h
- Partner stops getting paid month after cancellation
- No penalties for customer churn (volume risk is ours)

---

### DNA Data Integration

**What We Provide:**
- API endpoint with customer genetic data (ACTN3, ACE, COL1A1, PPARGC1A)
- Interpretation guide: "ACTN3 R/R = Power athlete, ACE D/D = Endurance, etc."
- Training recommendations per genotype

**What Partner Provides:**
- PT can view DNA data in their platform (or we provide dashboard)
- PT uses data to customize programs (not mandatory, but encouraged)
- PT documents which genetic insights they used (for our data tracking)

**Data Security:**
- GDPR Article 9 compliant (genetic data = special category)
- Customer consent required (checkbox: "Share DNA with PT partner")
- Data encryption in transit (TLS 1.3)
- Partner signs DPA (Data Processing Agreement)

---

### White-Label Branding

**Customer-Facing:**
- PT coaching appears as **"Genetic Wellness Labs PT Coaching"**
- Not "Pactster via GWL" (confusing)
- PT introduces themselves: "Hi, I'm Emma, your GWL PT coach"

**Behind the Scenes:**
- Partner can mention partnership in their portfolio (after 6 months)
- We mention partner in investor decks (with permission)

---

### Quality Standards

**Minimum Requirements:**
- PT certifications: SATS Academy, AFPT, IKSU, or international equivalent (NASM, ACE, ISSA)
- Customer satisfaction: **>4.5/5 stars** (measured quarterly)
- Response time: <24h for customer messages
- No-show rate: <5% (PT cancels <5% of scheduled sessions)

**Monitoring:**
- We survey customers after each session (1-5 stars + comment)
- Quarterly review meeting (us + partner)
- If satisfaction drops below 4.5 for 2 consecutive quarters → we can terminate

---

### Exclusivity

**Our Request (Non-Compete):**
- Partner will not offer DNA-based PT coaching to other Swedish DNA-testing companies
- Duration: **3 years** from contract start
- Geography: Sweden (partner can do international DNA-coaching)

**Partner Request (Likely):**
- We will not partner with other PT companies in Sweden for same service
- We agree: Yes (only 1 primary PT partner, but can have backup if primary fails)

---

### Contract Duration & Exit

**Initial Term:**
- 12 months minimum (both parties commit)

**Renewal:**
- Auto-renews for 12 months unless terminated (90 days notice)

**Early Termination (Either Party):**
- Material breach (e.g., partner quality drops, we don't pay)
- 60 days written notice

**Termination Impact:**
- Active customers can finish their current month
- We transition customers to backup partner or pause service
- Partner must return all customer data (DNA data, workout logs)

## 📊 FINANCIAL PROJECTIONS

### Year 1 (Month 12 Launch, 2 Months Active)

**Customers:**
- Target: 65 customers (10% av 650 totalt)
- Average: Month 12 (30 customers), Month 13 (65 customers ramped)

**Revenue (Our 30% Cut):**
- Month 12: 30 × 148 kr = 4,440 SEK
- Month 13-onwards: 65 × 148 kr = 9,620 SEK/mån

**Year 1 Total (2 months):** 4,440 + 9,620 = **14,060 SEK**
*(Note: Plan summary shows 19,240 assuming full 65 for 2 months - this is conservative)*

**COGS:**
- Platform cost: 20 kr/customer/mån
- Month 12: 30 × 20 = 600 SEK
- Month 13: 65 × 20 = 1,300 SEK
- **Total COGS: 1,900 SEK**

**Net Profit Year 1:** 14,060 - 1,900 = **12,160 SEK**

**Margin:** 86% (after partner revenue share already deducted)

---

### Year 2 (Full Year, Scale to 240 Customers)

**Customers:**
- Start: 65 (Month 13)
- Growth: +15 customers/month average
- End: 240 customers (20% av 1,200 totalt)

**Revenue (Our 30% Cut):**
- Average customers/mån: (65 + 240) / 2 = 152
- 152 × 148 kr × 12 mån = **270,144 SEK**

**COGS:**
- 152 × 20 kr × 12 mån = **36,480 SEK**

**Net Profit Year 2:** 270,144 - 36,480 = **233,664 SEK**

**Margin:** 86%

---

### Year 3 (Decision Point: In-House vs Partnership)

**Customers:**
- Target: 400 customers (20% av 2,000 totalt)

**Partnership Model (Continue):**
- Revenue (our 30%): 400 × 148 kr × 12 = **710,400 SEK**
- COGS: 400 × 20 kr × 12 = 96,000 SEK
- **Net profit: 614,400 SEK** (86% margin)

**In-House Model (Switch):**
- Revenue: 400 × 495 kr × 12 = **2,376,000 SEK** (100% ours)
- COGS:
  - PT salaries: 4 full-time PTs × 40K × 12 = 1,920K
  - Benefits (20%): 384K
  - Platform cost: 400 × 20 × 12 = 96K
  - **Total COGS: 2,400K**
- **Net profit: -24K** ❌ **LOSS!**

**Conclusion:** Partnership model still better at 400 customers!

**Break-Even for In-House:**
- Need ~550 customers to justify 4 PTs
- At 550: Revenue 3.3M, COGS 2.4M, profit 900K (vs 850K partnership)
- **Only switch to in-house if we hit 550+ PT customers** (Year 4+)

## 📚 APPENDIX: SAMPLE CONTRACT CLAUSES

### Revenue Share Clause

```
3.1 Revenue Distribution
For each Customer subscribing to the PT Coaching Service ("Service"),
the monthly subscription fee of SEK 495 shall be distributed as follows:

a) Partner receives: 70% (SEK 347) per Customer per month
b) Company receives: 30% (SEK 148) per Customer per month

Payments shall be made by Company to Partner within 15 business days
following the end of each calendar month, based on the number of active
Customers during that month.
```

---

### DNA Data Sharing Clause

```
5.1 Genetic Data Access
Company shall provide Partner with API access to Customer genetic data
("Genetic Data"), limited to the following SNPs: ACTN3, ACE, COL1A1,
PPARGC1A, and ADRB2.

5.2 Data Use Restrictions
Partner agrees to use Genetic Data solely for the purpose of personalizing
PT coaching services. Partner shall not:
a) Share Genetic Data with third parties without Customer consent
b) Use Genetic Data for marketing or research purposes
c) Retain Genetic Data after contract termination

5.3 GDPR Compliance
Partner acknowledges that Genetic Data constitutes "special category data"
under GDPR Article 9 and agrees to implement appropriate technical and
organizational measures to protect such data.
```

---

### Quality Standards Clause

```
7.1 Service Quality
Partner shall maintain an average Customer satisfaction rating of at least
4.5 out of 5 stars, measured quarterly via Customer surveys conducted by Company.

7.2 Remediation
If Partner's average rating falls below 4.5 for two consecutive quarters:
a) Company and Partner shall conduct a joint review meeting
b) Partner shall implement a remediation plan within 30 days
c) If rating remains below 4.5 for a third consecutive quarter, Company may
   terminate this Agreement with 30 days written notice
```

---

### Exclusivity Clause

```
9.1 Non-Compete (Partner)
During the Term and for 3 years thereafter, Partner shall not provide
DNA-based PT coaching services to any competitor of Company operating
in the Swedish market.

9.2 Non-Compete (Company)
During the Term, Company shall not engage any other PT coaching partner
in Sweden for substantially similar services, except as a backup provider
in case of Partner's breach or incapacity.
```
