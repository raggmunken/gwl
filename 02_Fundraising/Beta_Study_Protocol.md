## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**Purpose:** Validate that DNA-powered personalized supplementation leads to measurable health improvements.

**Design:**
- **81 beta customers** (our early bird cohort)
- **Blood tests before (Month 0) and after (Month 3)**
- **Track subjective (energi, fokus) and objective (blodmarkörer) outcomes**
- **Measure retention**: How many stay subscribed after 3, 6 months?

**Success Criteria:**
- ✅ **>60% show improvement** in ≥2 blodmarkörer = Science validated
- ✅ **>80% retention at Month 6** = Business model validated
- ❌ **<60% improvement OR <60% retention** = Pivot required

**Budget:** 200K SEK (already allocated in 5M seed round)

## STUDY OBJECTIVES

### Primary Objectives:

**1. Scientific Validation**
- **Question:** Do DNA-matched supplements improve biomarkers more than generic supplements?
- **Hypothesis:** Participants with VDR Fok1 ff-genotype + high-dose D-vitamin will show 40%+ improvement in serum D-vitamin levels vs. baseline

**2. Customer Retention Validation**

### Secondary Objectives:

**3. Subjective Outcome Validation**

- **Hypothesis:** >70% report improved energy/focus by Month 3

**4. Churn Drivers**
- **Question:** Why do customers cancel? (if they do)
- **Analysis:** Exit surveys for churned customers

---

## STUDY DESIGN

### Type:
**Prospective Observational Cohort Study**

- **Prospective:** We follow participants forward in time (not retrospective)
- **Observational:** No control group, no placebo (this is real-world validation, not RCT)
- **Cohort:** Single group of 81 beta customers

**Why Not RCT (Randomized Controlled Trial)?**
- **Cost:** RCT requires control group = 2× blood tests, 2× customers
- **Ethics:** Hard to justify giving placebo to paying customers
- **Purpose:** We're validating real-world outcomes, not proving causation

**Limitations:**
- No control group = can't prove causation
- Placebo effect may contribute to improvements
- Self-selection bias (early adopters may be more health-conscious)

## ANALYSIS PLAN

### Primary Analysis: Blood Biomarker Improvements

**Method:** Paired t-test (baseline vs Month 3)

**For each biomarker:**
- Calculate % change: (Month 3 - Baseline) / Baseline × 100%
- Test: Is % change significantly > 0?
- Success: >60% of participants show improvement in ≥2 markers

**Subgroup Analysis:**
- Participants with VDR Fok1 ff genotype: D-vitamin improvement >40%?
- Participants with MTHFR C677T TT genotype: Homocysteine ↓ >20%?

**Visualization:**
- Before/after plots for each biomarker
- Heatmap: Who improved in which markers?

---

### Secondary Analysis: Subjective Outcomes

**Method:** Paired t-test (baseline vs Month 3)

**For each outcome (energy, focus, recovery, sleep, health):**
- Calculate change: Month 3 - Baseline
- Test: Is change significantly > 0?
- Success: >70% report improvement in ≥2 areas

**Correlation Analysis:**
- Do blood biomarker improvements correlate with subjective improvements?
- Example: If D-vitamin ↑ 50%, does energy also improve?

## ETHICAL CONSIDERATIONS

### Informed Consent:

**Participants must consent to:**
1. **DNA testing** (genetic data collection)
2. **Blood tests** (medical data collection)
3. **Data usage for research** (anonymized, aggregated analysis)

**GDPR Article 9 Compliance:**
- Explicit consent required for genetic data
- Right to withdraw consent at any time
- Right to data deletion (GDPR "right to be forgotten")

### Data Privacy:

**Storage:**
- AWS Stockholm (ISO 27001 compliant)
- Encrypted at rest & in transit
- Access controls (only authorized personnel)

**Anonymization:**
- For research publication, all data anonymized
- No personally identifiable information (PII) in analysis

### Adverse Events:

**Monitoring:**
- Participants can report side effects via app/email
- Dietist reviews all adverse events
- If serious adverse event (SAE): Stop supplementation, refer to doctor

**Reporting:**
- Log all adverse events (even minor)
- Report SAEs to regulatory authorities if required
